== phase_054.png
0: The Mouse Cursor
1: The mouse next to your computer
2: moves the mouse cursor.
3: A golden mouse cursor shows that
4: there is something interesting here.
5: If the mouse cursor is grey, there
6: is nothing here.
7: If the mouse cursor is hatched,
8: you cannot enter anything.

== phase_055.png
0: The System Menu
1: Click on the icon to open
2: the menu.
3: Here you can read the help or
4: the project diary, or quit
5: the game.

== phase_056.png
0: The Inventory
1: The top edge of the screen shows
2: all the things you are carrying.
3: -
4: Often you have to take things
5: before you can use them.

== phase_057.png
0: The Command Line
1: Often it shows nothing at all.
2: If the mouse cursor points at an
3: object or a person, its name is
4: shown.
5: When you give a command,
6: it is shown here.

== phase_058.png
0: Walking & Looking
1: Click the right mouse button on
2: the floor and the player walks
3: there.
4: Or use the cursor keys to
6: steer the player.
7: Click the right mouse button on
8: an object and the player looks
9: at it.

== phase_059.png
0: Using Objects
1: Using, taking and reading objects
2: and talking to people is all done
3: with a single click.
4: To do one of these things, click
5: the left mouse button on an
6: object or a person.
7: -

== phase_060.png
0: Combining Objects
1: You must own at least one of
2: the two things.
3: Click on the first one in the
4: inventory and hold the button.
5: Then drag the mouse cursor
6: to the second object.
7: Release the mouse button there.

== phase_061.png
0: Moving Things
1: Things you cannot take with you
2: can sometimes be moved.
3: -
4: Click on the object and
5: hold the button down.
6: Then drag the mouse in the
7: direction you want.
8: Only then let go of the mouse.

== phase_062.png
0: The System Menu
1: Press the F10 key to open
2: the system menu.
3: -
4: It has the settings for the
5: music volume, the animation
6: speed and the speech speed.
7: -
8: Pick a setting with the 'Up'
9: and 'Down' keys. 'Left' and
11 x=106: 'Right' change it.
12: -

== phase_063.png
0: Loading & Saving
1: F5 opens the save menu, F7
2: the load menu.
3: Every saved game has a
4: snapshot and a short
5: description.
6: Bottom right is a game that
7: was saved automatically.

== phase_064.png
0: Spoken Text
1: When a person says something,
2: it appears above them and
3: disappears after a while.
4: As long as you hold down the
5: shift key, the text stays.
6: You can read it in peace.
7: Release the shift key and the
8: text disappears at once.
9: You don't have to wait.

== phase_065.png
0: Text Windows
1: Text windows show things that
2: are not (yet) spoken.
3: Text windows pause the game.
4: A click with the left mouse
5: button closes them.
6: Sometimes, e.g. in dialogues,
7: you can choose something in
8: text windows.
